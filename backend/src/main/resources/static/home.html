How It Works
Simple 4-step process from request to delivery
1

Post a Request

Customer posts a transport request with pickup, drop, item details and budget

2

Shippers Bid

Multiple shippers compete by placing bids with price and delivery time

3

Compare Bids

All bids are visible to everyone in real time — lowest bid is highlighted

4

Award Winner

Customer selects the best bid and a confirmed order is created automatically
